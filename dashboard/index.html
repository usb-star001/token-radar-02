How the price is set

Blind tests — every term tried, only the curve kept

ModelLeave-one-out errorKept

Leave-one-out error is the mean absolute error in log market cap when each traded token is held out and predicted from the rest. Lower is better. Category, growth, age and chain count each make the held-out error worse, so each stays out of the price — the board still shows growth and age on every row, and the farm plan still uses age for its hold.

The farm math

Each plan sizes its deposit from $100, $250, $500 or $1,000: the smallest that clears the low end of the comparables’ band is a FARM; the smallest that clears only the central estimate is a THIN; a row no deposit up to $1,000 clears is a SKIP. Expected value is pro-rata: 10% of the implied value (the share a launch hands its farmers — Arbitrum handed its users 11.62% of supply) times the deposit’s share of the protocol’s TVL. Cost is six transactions at the cheapest chain’s typical gas (November 2026), a $2 bridge fee, and the deposit’s opportunity cost at 5% a year over the hold. The low end is the 25th percentile of what traded tokens actually fetched against the curve.

What this radar does not know

The curve explains 46% of the variance in log market cap across the 110 traded tokens. The band is wide — a quarter of traded tokens fetch under 0.35× the curve and a quarter fetch over 3.06× it — so every implied value carries a band and every rank carries a range from 200 refits. A protocol on this board may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry, and a launch may never come.
